# Calder County — Department of Household Services

## Legal Services Directive LS-2026/04

**To:** Head of Digital Services  
**Date:** 14 July 2026  
**Subject:** Mandatory additional data collection, form HSP-1  

---

Following the Assistance Appeals Panel's decision in *Re Hollis* and the County Attorney's advice of last month, form HSP-1 must collect two additional items. Both are **mandatory** and both must be in place before any further applications are processed.

### 1. Identity verification for every adult household member

For the applicant **and every household member aged 16 or over**, the form must capture:

- the type of identity document produced (state ID, passport, permanent resident card, tribal ID, or other), and
- the document's reference number.

Where a household member has no acceptable document, the form must record that fact and the reason, and the case must be flagged for supervisor referral.

### 2. Prior assistance in another jurisdiction

Every applicant must be asked whether any household member has received assistance from an equivalent program in another county or state **in the preceding 24 months**.

Where the answer is yes, the form must capture the jurisdiction, the program name, and the approximate dates. Where the answer is not known, that must be recordable — an applicant may genuinely not know what a household member claimed elsewhere.

---

### The constraint

The County Attorney is aware that HSP-1 already has a completion rate of 21.8%. Legal Services' position is that these fields are non-negotiable, and that **any fall in the completion rate as a result of adding them will be treated as a failure of implementation rather than an unavoidable consequence.**

You are expected to add them without regressing completion.
